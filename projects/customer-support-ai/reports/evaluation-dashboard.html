Engineering decisions

Model authority is bounded. Interpretation may be probabilistic; access control and policy applicability are deterministic.

Missing context is a valid outcome. The assistant asks for purchase details instead of guessing a policy.

Operational status comes from tools. Order data is returned only after authentication and customer scoping.

Uncertainty can become work. Tickets preserve the case for a human rather than ending at a refusal.

Evidence limits

Fictional data and policies

Local function-level evaluator
